# nanos world - Version 0.22.0

- **Release Date:** 2020-06-01 10:49:11 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2020-06-01T10:49:11.885Z`

---

This update revamps our utility classes (**Rotator/Vector/Color/Vector2D**). They were remade and now are part of the native Lua environment. I.E.:  We can now have functions and do operations `+`, `-`, `*`, `==` and etc with them, and better part: they are open sourced at https://github.com/nanos-world/nanos-world-lua-lib. Please feel free to collaborate who's good at Math or wants to make it better.

**New Features**
*  New **Utility Classes** functions (https://docs.nanos.world/scripting/utility/index.html).
*  New **MathLibrary** (https://docs.nanos.world/scripting/utility/NanosMathLibrary.html).

**Improvements**
*  **Utility Classes** have much more performance now, and can be used freely on the scripting without restrictions anymore.

**Bug Fixes**
*  Fixed `Weapon.BaseDamage` not working.
*  Fixed some props/assets not returning physical superficie when being shot/hit (for triggering sounds and particles properly).
*  Fixed bad animations while handling a weapon and jumping/swimming.
