# nanos world - Version 0.15.0

- **Release Date:** 2020-05-18 10:20:48 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2020-05-18T10:20:48.319Z`

---

**New Features**
*  **Parachute** now works and appears properly!
*  `AttachTo(Actor, Location)` was changed to `AttachTo(Actor, Bone, Location)`. For all Character's bones, please check https://docs.nanos.world/scripting/classes/Character.html#character-s-skeleton-bone-names. **Please Scripters update your code.**
*  Now **Deaths** have a better reaction to the shots and will animate and react properly (characters will no longer simply fall straight into ragdoll).

**Bug Fixes**
*  **Grenade** works properly again!
*  Fixed **Blood Holes** appearing weird with a translucent border.
*  Fixed some **Flowers** becoming black under shadows.
