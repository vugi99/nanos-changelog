# nanos world - Version 1.164.0

- **Release Date:** 2026-10-24 11:45:00 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2026-10-24T11:45:00Z`

---

### ⏫ Improvements
- Improved rotation precision sync: fixing stepped **Control Rotation** on other players, and `SetRotation()` and Rotators/Quats sent through remote events.

### 🔨 Bug Fixes
- Fixed crashes when a native Lua function raised an error.
- Fixed `SetScale()` sending scales of `0.01` or smaller as zero, it now uses a `0.001` precision.
- Fixed `Player:Spectate()` attaching the camera to the Character root instead of its head.
- Fixed spectating players on **Vehicles** not working.
- Fixed `Follow()` causing NPCs to slow their velocity when reaching the target.
- Fixed cables not syncing visibility properly.
