# nanos world - Version 0.23.0

- **Release Date:** 2020-06-05 11:10:28 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2020-06-05T11:10:28.72Z`

---

This update adds the incredible possibility of including custom **Assets** into your server!! We've also reworked our https://docs.nanos.world/getting_started/ and it has much more information than before (including **Assets Tutorials**).

**New Features**
*  **Custom Assets!** Server owners can now import custom Assets in their servers, the assets will be transferred like the scripts and can be referenced in the lua code.
*  Our loading screen was improved to show a downloading progress bar.
