# nanos world - Version 1.166.1

- **Release Date:** 2026-10-28 14:30:00 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2026-10-28T14:30:00Z`

---

### 🆕 New Features
- Player profiles can now be opened from the in-game player list, showing teams and badges, as well as your own account, achievements and stats.

### ⏫ Improvements
- The **Server Browser** now sorts compatible servers first, followed by lower ping and higher player counts. Search also matches game modes and maps.
- Vehicle interaction tooltips now indicate whether you will enter as a driver or passenger.
- The **Hide UI** keybinding now also hides the Canvas.
- Improved physics hit sounds and their cooldowns.
- The hardware benchmark now selects upscaling, latency reduction and hardware ray tracing settings based on supported hardware and benchmark results.
- Water vehicles now spawn with 1000 health by default, matching wheeled vehicles.
- Water vehicles now reverse at half thrust, use improved automatic pontoon placement, and only block throttle when every pontoon is fully submerged.
- [`VehicleWater:SetThrustStrength()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/vehiclewater#function-setthruststrength) now uses Newtons, with a default of `3000`.
- Improved the error shown when a Vault token is invalid.
- Improved welcome and goodbye messages.

### 🔨 Bug Fixes
- Fixed camera auto re-aligning while in a water vehicle camera due to vertical movement on waves.
- Fixed water vehicles with differently oriented meshes floating at an angle.
- Fixed buoyancy calculations for water vehicles with an offset center of mass.
- Fixed [`VehicleWater:SetThrustStrength()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/vehiclewater#function-setthruststrength) not working and being overwritten every frame.
- Fixed a crash after loading a map when the local player controller was unavailable.
- Fixed cables spawned with visuals disabled still being visible.
- Fixed a crash when possessing a character during Unpossess.
- Fixed a crash when calling an entity method on an entity of the wrong class.
- Fixed a crash when an inherited constructor replaces its `__entity`.
- Fixed local packages being added to their team again on every Vault refresh.
- Fixed saving your profile erroring with empty optional email.
- Fixed popups appearing behind other UI elements.
