# nanos world - Version 1.7.1

- **Release Date:** 2022-04-29 16:27:56 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2022-04-29T16:27:56.349Z`

---

**New Features**

* Added new default Maps: `EmptyMap` and `OceanMap`.
* Added new default Basic shape Props.
* New auto Water Pontoons system.
* Enabled Sprint while flying.


**Improvements**

* Improved Floor material on default maps.
* Improved Water Physics from Props and floating objects, they all are normalized now (more improvements soon).
* Improved Grabbing Props physics.


**Bug Fixes**

* Fixed Bullet hitting Pawn while dead.
* Fixed Locked animation shaking head.
* Fixed crash when spawning an Actor with NaN Vector.
* Fixed MaterialScalarParameters not setting properly.
* Fixed a crash when spawning assets with double slashes.
* Fixed Characters animation hit reaction on hitting the Pelvis.
* Improved Ragdoll hit detection (when being hit).
* Fixed offset when entering vehicle crouched/proning.
* Fixed leaving vehicle in wrong side when flipped.
* Fixed collision when entering flying mode while driving.
* Fixed being stuck while standing up (from ragdoll) after dropping a weapon.
* Attempt to fix Swimming mode forever after leaving water body.
* Fixed Chaos vehicle reusing configs from other spawned vehicle (painful).
* Fixed entering vehicle while dead.
* Fixed VOIP not working.
