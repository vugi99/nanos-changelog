# nanos world - Version 1.171.2

- **Release Date:** 2026-11-17 15:00:00 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2026-11-17T15:00:00Z`

---

### 🆕 New Features
- Added methods to get a Player's local VOIP settings: [`GetVOIPLocalVolume()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoiplocalvolume), [`GetVOIPLocalMaxDistance()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoiplocalmaxdistance), [`GetVOIPLocalHighPassFilter()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoiplocalhighpassfilter), [`GetVOIPLocalLowPassFilter()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoiplocallowpassfilter), [`GetVOIPLocalOcclusion()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoiplocalocclusion) and [`GetVOIPLocalAttenuation()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoiplocalattenuation).
- Added methods to get a Player's global VOIP settings: [`GetVOIPGlobalVolume()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoipglobalvolume), [`GetVOIPGlobalHighPassFilter()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoipglobalhighpassfilter) and [`GetVOIPGlobalLowPassFilter()`](https://docs.nanos-world.com/docs/next/scripting-reference/classes/player#function-getvoipgloballowpassfilter).

### ⏫ Improvements
- Sounds now fade based on their distance to the possessed Pawn instead of the camera, while panning still follows the camera. Spectators continue to use the camera for distance.
- Local VOIP's inner attenuation radius can now reach the maximum distance, instead of being limited to half of it.

### 🌐 Account, Store & Website
- Consolidated creation actions into a **+ New** menu on profile and team management pages.
- Support replies now warn when a new message or status change arrives while you are writing.
- Added a new selector for tag and language with searchable dropdowns, removable selections and suggestions.
- Improved form validation so an emptied field does not show a required-field error until submission.
- Improved the Store category filter: selecting a category again clears it and shows all categories.
