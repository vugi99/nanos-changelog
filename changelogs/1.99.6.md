# nanos world - Version 1.99.6

- **Release Date:** 2025-12-30 18:18:25 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2025-12-30T18:18:25.483Z`

---

### 🆕 New Features
- The **Vault** has been completely overhauled and is now fully integrated with the Store, unlocking a wide range of new features and workflows directly from the in-game menu.
- You can now **Rate and Review** any Vault resource. Ratings and comments are displayed both in the Reviews tab and directly under the resource name.
- It is now possible to **Create Teams** directly from the menu. Teams can have their name, description, and logo edited, and can be created either from your Profile screen or during resource creation.
- The **Vault Manage** tab has been fully redesigned. You can now edit resource metadata (title, description, logo, screenshots, visibility and tags) and manage versions, including uploading, setting changelogs, releasing, deleting, and modifying version numbers, all from the manage tab.
- You can now **Create a Store Resource** directly from an existing local resource within the game. Simply go to the Manage tab and select the Create option.
- Introduced a new **Team Pop-up**. Clicking on a resource's team now opens a pop-up showing the team description, members, and all associated resources.
- The **Profile** screen has been improved. You can now edit your username directly in-game, and view your role and all teams you belong to.

### ⏫ Improvements
- Improved **Vault filtering**, allowing you to filter by all available tags or restrict results to resources from your own teams. Existing filtering bugs have also been fixed.
- Clicking on a Vault resource dependency now opens a pop-up with detailed information about that resource.
- Enabled the **OpenXR** plugin.

### 🔨 Bug Fixes
- Fixed a crash that occurred when closing the game using ALT+F4.
