### ⏫ Improvements

- Now Custom **Loading Screens** will appear way earlier in the loading stage.

- Updated **Sentry** to 1.9.1.

### 🔨 Bug Fixes
- Fixed **Character Physical Animations** not being re-applied after the Character respawn.
- Fixed **CharacterSimple** resetting the relative mesh location when respawning.
- Fixed Crash when non-UE4 Skeleton Character tries to use a Weapon/Melee.
- Fixed **Draw Debug Vehicle Doors** and **Character Capsules** not working correctly.
