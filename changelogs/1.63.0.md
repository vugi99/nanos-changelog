# nanos world - Version 1.63.0

- **Release Date:** 2023-11-02 17:07:25 UTC
- **Raw Timestamp:** `2023-11-02T17:07:25.000Z`

---

🆕 **New Features**
- End of Halloween Event!
- The game has been updated to Unreal Engine 5.3! Please help us testing the game and updating your assets! Note: The TestingMap is known to have issues when having Lumen disabled (landscape and water invisible).
- We got two new events for **Server**: [`Restart`](https://docs.nanos.world/docs/next/scripting-reference/static-classes/server#event-restart) and [`ChangeMap`](https://docs.nanos.world/docs/next/scripting-reference/static-classes/server#event-changemap)!

⏫ **Improvements**
- Updated CEF to the latest version!

🔨 **Bug Fixes**
- Fixed client side entities being destroyed when an entity was being destroyed while other client-side entities were attached to it.
- Fixed Server.GetMap() returning new map before all entities were being destroyed.
